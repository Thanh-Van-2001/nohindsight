Why not just copy the leaderboard?

Leaderboards rank traders by what already happened. Across all 47,220 wallets on Hyperliquid's leaderboard, records picked with hindsight backtest at a Sharpe of 3.4; picked the way a copier must, before the fact, 1.4. And about 1 in 3 leaderboard accounts stop trading within a year. The ranking is marketing. The filter is the product.

How it works

  1. Screen. Strategies and Hyperliquid wallets are scored on past data only, with fees, funding and delayed fills included.
  2. Diversify. Survivors are combined into baskets, so one leader going quiet doesn't end your plan.
  3. Copy in your account. Connect a trade-only API key (no withdrawal rights). Orders go into your own exchange account.
